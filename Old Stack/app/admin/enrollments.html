Enrollments

An enrollment ties a student to one teacher for one subject — it’s what billing cycles hang off. Teachers request them and you approve; you can also enroll a student directly. Deactivating keeps billing through the current cycle, then stops (that’s the “ending” state), so nothing is cut off mid-cycle.
